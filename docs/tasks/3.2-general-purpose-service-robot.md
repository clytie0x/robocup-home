## How to read a row

`kind` is `official`, `derived`, or `assumption`.
`result` is `not-run` for every row. `pass` and `fail` need an executable test and its output. None exists.
`gate` is `sim` or `hardware`.
`block` `open` means the PDF leaves two readings or an absent definition. This row cannot pass while it stays open.
A missing coordinate, clock unit, or retry count is an `assumption` row. A later simulation may label a value. That label is not a printed measurement.

## Printed score rows

The sheet prints two positive lines, `3×400` and `3×100`. It prints the total 1500. It does not print the products 1200 and 300. Those products are the arithmetic below, and 1200 + 300 = 1500. Penalty lines are outside that sum. The column "In the printed total" records that arithmetic. It is not a printed column.

Section 2.3.3 allows an outstanding bonus of up to 10 percent of the maximum test score, and the Technical Committee decides whether to award any of it. The sheet prints 150 on the outstanding line. One tenth of the printed total 1500 is 150. The PDF does not print that multiplication. The award is not automatic.
